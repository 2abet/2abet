# Akinyemi Arabambi

Applied AI researcher and technical founder in Wolverhampton, UK. I study why learned policies misbehave and how to keep deterministic control around them.

- Led industrial reinforcement learning research at Magna Specialist Confectioners (multi-agent PPO for automated guided vehicles, 2025 to 2026). The reward got exploited in three different ways before it worked; the fixes and the guard around the policy taught me more than the result.
- First author of *Decision Alignment Protocols: Harmonising AI Agents for Comprehensive Market Assessment*, IEEE ICECCE 2025. [doi:10.1109/ICECCE67514.2025.11257956](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICECCE67514.2025.11257956)
- Founder of [Bambi's Group Ltd](https://bambisgroup.co.uk), which ships LLM systems for industrial clients on one rule: the model advises, rules decide.
- Lecturer in computational mathematics, programming and systems architecture at the University of Wolverhampton International Study Centre.

Most of my industrial code is private. Public repositories worth opening:

- [TrainTime](https://github.com/2abet/TrainTime): Keras callback that estimates remaining training time. On [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/train-time/).
- [genai_scaffold](https://github.com/2abet/genai_scaffold): CLI that starts a generative AI project with a sane structure. On [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/genai-scaffold/).
- [py-data-rag-demo](https://github.com/2abet/py-data-rag-demo): fully local RAG with LlamaIndex, ChromaDB and Ollama; every answer comes with the passages it was built from.
